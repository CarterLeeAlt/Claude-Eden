Eden UI is a derived private font for horizontal Windows controls and NSIS.
Inter supplies its supported characters; Noto Sans SC supplies the remaining
characters. The derived family is named Eden UI and remains licensed under
SIL Open Font License 1.1. Both source copyright notices are embedded in it.
Rebuild regular and bold faces with scripts/build-native-fonts.py using
FontTools 4.68.0. Original fonts are the inputs; no system font is used at runtime.
